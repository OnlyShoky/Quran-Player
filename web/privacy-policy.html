1. Introduction & Overview

sukun (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a calm, minimalist Quran audio application developed by Arakat. sukun is available as a web application at https://sukun.arakat.app and as a mobile application for Android (Application ID: com.arakat.sukun).

We believe that listening to the Holy Qur'an should be peaceful, pure, and completely free from distractions and intrusive data collection. This Privacy Policy describes how sukun handles data, the third-party audio services we use, and your rights when using the application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

sukun is built with a strict privacy-by-design architecture:

  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need an account, email address, password, or profile to use the app.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, record, collect, or store your name, email, phone number, address, contacts, or calendar.
  • No Device Sensors or Media Access: The app does not access your camera, microphone, photo gallery, location, or biometric data.
  • No Advertisements: sukun contains zero advertisements. We do not integrate ad networks (such as Google AdMob) or advertising identifiers (AAID / IDFA). We will never sell, lease, or monetize your data.

3. Information We Collect or Process

Depending on the platform you use (Web or Android), limited non-personal technical data is processed as follows:

A. Aggregate Analytics (Web Version)

On our website (sukun.arakat.app), we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with IP anonymization to monitor aggregate, anonymous visitor trends (such as total daily listeners, popular languages, and device screen size). This data is purely statistical and cannot be used to identify individuals. No advertising, remarketing, or cross-site tracking features are enabled.

B. Local Storage & Device Preferences

To preserve your listening setup across sessions, sukun saves your settings locally on your device (using Android SharedPreferences and Web localStorage). This includes:

  • Your chosen visual theme (Light, Dark, or System)
  • Your selected application language (English, Spanish, French, or Arabic)
  • Your favorite and pinned reciters
  • Your current playback playlist and selected audio streaming API
  • Your UI view preference (mosaic grid or list view)

This data remains strictly stored on your own device and is never transmitted to, synced with, or stored on our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or resetting app storage in Android settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Audio APIs

sukun streams audio recitations directly from trusted Islamic audio CDNs and API providers. When you play a Surah, your device establishes a direct HTTP/HTTPS connection with the selected content provider to retrieve the audio file and reciter metadata:

  • MP3Quran.net: Catalog and high-quality audio servers (mp3quran.net).
  • QuranicAudio.com: Curated high-fidelity audio collection hosted by Quran Foundation (quranicaudio.com).
  • AlQuran Cloud: Global Islamic audio and metadata CDN operated by Islamic Network (alquran.cloud).

Like any web request on the internet, your device transmits standard network headers (such as your public IP address and device User-Agent) directly to these third-party audio servers so they can deliver the audio stream to your player. These providers operate under their respective privacy terms and industry-standard server logging practices.

Voluntary Support / Donations

Users who wish to support the project can choose to make a voluntary contribution via external links to PayPal or Buy Me a Coffee. All payments are processed entirely on those third-party platforms. sukun does not process, receive, or store any payment or financial details.

5. Android Device Permissions

The Android version of sukun (com.arakat.sukun) requests only the minimal technical permissions required to operate as an audio player:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to stream Quran recitations from the audio APIs mentioned above.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required on Android 14+ so that audio playback continues without interruption when you switch apps or turn off your screen.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display the system media playback controls (play, pause, previous, next) in your notification shade and lock screen. We never send promotional or spam push notifications.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Ensures continuous audio playback without the operating system prematurely cutting off the audio stream while your phone is locked.

6. Children's Privacy

sukun is intended for people of all ages, including children and families seeking to listen to and memorize the Holy Qur'an.

Because our app collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions), in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Security & Data Protection

All communications between sukun, our web domain, and third-party audio APIs are conducted over industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption to ensure that your traffic cannot be tampered with or intercepted in transit.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Under regulations such as GDPR and CCPA, users have rights regarding their personal data (access, rectification, erasure). Because sukun holds no personal data, accounts, or user records on any server, there is no database record for us to retrieve or delete.

You have complete, direct control over your on-device preferences at all times:

  • On Android: Go to Settings → Apps → sukun → Storage → Clear Data to wipe all saved preferences.
  • On Web: Clear your browser's local storage and cookies for sukun.arakat.app.
